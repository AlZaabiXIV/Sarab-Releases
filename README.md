## 📖 The story

**Year 2050.** In a single lifetime, the Gulf rose from desert sands into the most advanced region on Earth — solar-powered megacities, flying transit, AI-run infrastructure and a space programme reaching for Mars.

Not everyone celebrated.

**The Obsidian Pact**, a shadowy empire built on stolen technology and scavenged war machines, wants what the Gulf has built — and it's ready to take it by force.

Standing in its way: the **GCC Coalition**. Six nations, one shield, led by the **United Arab Emirates** — and armed with a secret weapon the world has never seen:

### 🌫️ **SARAB — the Mirage.**
Holographic armies that aren't there. Bases that vanish. Drones you never see coming.
*In this war, nothing is what it seems.*

## ⚔️ Features

| | |
|---|---|
| 🏗️ **Classic base-building, reborn** | Your construction drones **3D-print** every building out of desert sand, layer by layer |
| 💎 **Desert wealth** | Harvest **Rimal Silica** from the sands and rare **Bio-Pearls** from the sea — a tribute to the Gulf's pearl-diving heritage |
| 🤖 **2050 warfare** | Exosuit soldiers, robot hounds, falcon drones, drone swarms, hover tanks and laser defences |
| 🌫️ **Mirage tech** | Decoy armies, cloaking fields and illusion warfare — outsmart, don't just outgun |
| 🇦🇪🇸🇦🇶🇦🇰🇼🇧🇭🇴🇲 **Six allied nations** | Pick your nation for unique colours and a signature unit |
| 🗣️ **Khaleeji soul** | Units answer in Gulf dialect — *"أبشر!"* — with original Khaleeji-industrial music |
| 🥽 **VR war-table** | Hover above the battlefield like a giant — grab buildings, pick up tanks, command with your hands *(planned)* |
| 🔗 **PC ↔ VR cross-play** | Challenge a friend whether they're on a PC or in a headset *(planned)* |
| 📼 **Classic Mode** | One toggle turns the game into a 90s-style retro experience — pure nostalgia |

## 🛡️ Factions

### 🟢 The GCC Coalition *(playable)*
Led by the UAE, united with Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman. High-tech, precise, masters of the Mirage.

**Signature units (preview):**
- **Fursan** — exosuit infantry
- **Saluki** — robotic hound, fast and lethal
- **Saqr** — robotic falcon scout
- **Sirb** — drone-swarm carrier
- **Haboob** — hover tank that rides the dunes
- **Shams** — laser air defence

### ⚫ The Obsidian Pact *(enemy)*
A fictional empire of scavenged war machines and brute force.

### 🔴 IFRIT *(coming later)*
A rogue AI that answers to no one — and hacks everyone.

> *All enemy factions are entirely fictional.*

## 📥 Download & install

> 🛠️ **The game is currently in development — no public build yet.**

When builds are available:
1. Click **[Releases](../../releases)** on the right side of this page.
2. Under the **latest version**, open **Assets**.
3. Download the file for your platform (**PC** installer or **Quest** package).
4. Run the installer and follow the steps.

The game checks this page automatically and **tells you when an update is ready**.
